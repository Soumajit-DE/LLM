Employee Leave Policy

Annual Leave: 20 days per calendar year. Unused leave can be carried forward (max 5 days).
Sick Leave: 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.
Casual Leave: 6 days per year for personal matters; cannot be carried forward.
Application: Submit requests via the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance.
Emergency Leave: Up to 2 days for unforeseen circumstances; notify your manager on the same day.
